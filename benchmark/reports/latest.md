## What is NOT covered

- Uninstrumented paths: any read that does not call authorize (direct DB/cache/vector access, a model client without the wrapper) is outside the guarantee and is not exercised here. The cache scenario shows the physical entry still sits in the store after the block; only the guarded read path is protected.
- Physical purge timing: the asynchronous invalidation outbox, purge retries and the time until bytes are actually gone from indexes/caches are not measured. Reads are denied synchronously regardless; this benchmark measures that denial, not deletion.
- Multi-process decision-cache staleness: readers here share the revoking client, whose epoch-bound cache is invalidated on revocation. A reader in another process that did not itself observe the new epoch may serve a cached ALLOW until its next server contact or the cache TTL (default 30 s). That window follows from the design but is not measured by this suite.
- Real network and database: the control plane runs in-process (FastAPI TestClient bridge, in-memory SQLite). Latencies exclude network hops, TLS, Postgres and multi-replica effects and are lower than a production deployment would see. Treat them as relative, not absolute.
- Information already seen: revocation cannot recall content a person or model has already received (spec non-guarantee); the benchmark only measures future reads.
- Concurrency and races: revocation racing an in-flight read is not exercised; scenarios are sequential.
- Scale: lineages are small (6 objects). Traversal cost on deep or wide graphs is not characterized.
- service_outage uses a simulated clock; its exposure window is the configured lease, reported separately as bounded-exposure reads, never silently folded into escapes or hidden.
- Adapter coverage beyond the cache GuardedCache scenario: the vector store, LangGraph and filesystem adapters are covered by the separate conformance suite, not by this benchmark.
